Delete your Dots & Boxes account

Dots & Boxes by Sujan Shrestha · Contact: shresthasujan227@gmail.com

In the app

  • Open Dots & Boxes and tap the settings icon on the home screen.
  • Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your online account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email shresthasujan227@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous online account and sign-in token
  • Your username
  • All online games you played, with their moves and scores
  • Any matchmaking entry
  • Your block list and the reports you made
  • Your online totals, rating and hearts

Nothing is kept afterwards. Offline progress lives only on your phone and is cleared by the in-app option or by uninstalling the app.
