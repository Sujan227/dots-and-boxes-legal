Privacy Policy

Dots & Boxes · Effective 2 October 2026

This policy explains what the Dots & Boxes app ("the app") collects, why, and how to delete it. The app is made by Sujan Shrestha, Kathmandu, Nepal ("we"). Contact: shresthasujan227@gmail.com

In short

  • Playing against the computer or Pass & Play sends nothing off your phone.
  • Online play uses an anonymous account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • You can delete your online account and all its games at any time from Settings in the app.

Offline play

Your username, your campaign progress, your settings (theme, sound and music) and the result of the online age check (only "13 or older: yes or no", never your birth date) are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Online play

When you first play online, the app creates an anonymous account with our hosting provider and stores:

  • a random account ID and a sign-in token (kept in your phone's secure keystore),
  • your username, and when it was created or last changed,
  • your online games: board settings, moves, scores, result, room codes and times,
  • a matchmaking entry while you are looking for an opponent (board settings and times; removed when matched or cancelled).

Opponents can see your username and the moves of games you play with them. Nobody else can see your games.

Like any internet service, our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic request logs to run and protect the service. These logs are kept only for a short period and are not used to identify you.

Why we use it

Only to run online play: matching players, enforcing the rules and turn timer, keeping usernames unique and preventing cheating and abuse. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure. We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles.

Who processes it

Online data is stored by Supabase (supabase.com), our hosting provider, in its Mumbai, India region. Supabase processes it only on our behalf. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

Online data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account also removes every game you played, including from your opponents' history.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete my account. This immediately and permanently erases your online account, username, games and matchmaking entry, and clears the username and progress on your phone.
  • Without the app: email shresthasujan227@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We delete it within 30 days. Because accounts are anonymous, the username is all we need.
  • Uninstalling the app alone does not delete online data.

Children

Online play is only for people aged 13 or older. The app asks for your birth month and year before online play; if you are under 13, online play stays switched off and nothing is sent. Playing offline is fine at any age and collects nothing. If we learn that a child under 13 has an online account, we delete it. Parents can contact us at shresthasujan227@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can change your username in the app and delete your account yourself; for anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

Your sign-in token is kept in the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. The server checks every move and only lets you read your own games. No system is perfectly secure, but we only keep the minimum data needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
